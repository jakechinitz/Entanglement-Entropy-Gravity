Supplement: Group Field / Tensor Underpinnings of the Entropic Scalar EFT

Author: Jake Chinitz Type: Microscopic supplement

This supplement develops microscopic underpinnings for the macroscopic $S_{\text{ent}}$ field using group-field and tensor-network constructions. Spin-network–like degrees of freedom are coarse-grained over layers to yield an area-law entanglement capacity corrected by diffusive channel sharing, motivating the universal $+1/2+1/2+1/2$ sharing exponent in the renormalization of $\kappa_m$ and introducing the wedge-sharing factor $g_{\text{share}}$ in the normalization.
