Derivations and Numerical Validations for the Entropic Scalar EFT

Author: Jake Chinitz Type: Technical derivations & numerics

This technical companion fixes the covariant action, equations of motion, and stress–energy for $S_{\text{ent}}$, and establishes units and normalizations unambiguously. A top-down derivation of $\kappa_m$ runs from the Planck scale to the electron's Compton scale with total exponent $5/2$ (area law plus three $+1/2$ sharing factors), matching the electron calibration at the sub-percent level. Disk solutions yield $\Delta S(R) \propto [1+\ln(R/R_d)]$ and, via a linearized entropic force, recover Baryonic Tully–Fisher with the correct slope and physically set zero-point.
