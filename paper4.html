Theoretical Foundations of the Wedge-Sharing Constant g_share in Emergent Gravity

Author: Jake Chinitz Type: g_share foundations

This paper explains, in plain terms and with closed-form geometry, how the wedge-sharing constant that normalizes the entanglement-scalar theory is pinned down. A cone on the sphere sets a baseline spread per step, while realistic transport has a modest persistence that boosts the baseline by a fixed multiplier. The product of these contributions lands naturally at $g_{\text{share}} \approx \ln(1680)$, with a small script scanning cone sizes and persistence to show where $\sim 7.4$ appears within conservative upper bounds.
