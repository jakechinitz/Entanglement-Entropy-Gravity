PURE CONDENSATE

Unified Entanglement-Entropy Gravity

An Entropic Scalar EFT in Six Papers

We present an emergent/entropic gravity framework in which quantum entanglement entropy $S_{\text{ent}}$ generates spacetime curvature and derives Newton's constant $G$ from first principles. A single scalar field encodes vacuum entanglement deficits, linking mass–information equivalence to galactic dynamics (flat rotation curves, Baryonic Tully–Fisher). We fix $G$ de novo via a wedge-sharing constant $g_{\text{share}} \approx 7.4$ motivated by group-field/tensor constructions and transport geometry, then validate units, normalizations, and the electron 1-bit anchor with analytic derivations and numerics.

Key Program Claim: Gravity is an entanglement-mediated response: once the mass–information constant $\kappa_m$ and a single geometric sharing factor $g_{\text{share}}$ are fixed, the framework predicts Newton's $G$, the MOND/RAR scale $a_0$, and the lepton curvature with no astrophysical free parameters.

Layperson Overview

What is this theory about?

The core idea is that gravity is not a fundamental force but the macroscopic response of an underlying entanglement medium. A single real scalar field $S_{\text{ent}}(x)$ encodes local deficits of vacuum entanglement. Gradients in this field behave like "entanglement pressure," sourcing curvature in much the same way that stress–energy sources curvature in general relativity.

Mass is identified with information through a universal conversion constant $\kappa_m$ (kg/bit). In the simplest operational picture, a free electron carries exactly one bit of vacuum-subtracted entanglement across a spatial cut. Once that 1-bit anchor is fixed, the same $\kappa_m$ determines how much curvature corresponds to a given entropy deficit.

Key Objects

  • $S_{\text{ent}}(x)$: entanglement-deficit scalar field.
  • $\kappa_m$: mass–information constant (kg/bit).
  • $g_{\text{share}}$: wedge-sharing constant from transport geometry.
  • $a_0$: emergent acceleration scale in galaxy dynamics.
  • Spin-3 condensate: bosonic microstructure of the entanglement medium.

What problems is it trying to solve?

The program targets several puzzles at once:

Target Problems

  • Why is $G$ what it is? In standard physics, Newton's constant is an empirical input. Here, $G$ is derived from the combination of black-hole entropy, the entanglement-scalar EFT, and a single geometric constant $g_{\text{share}}$.
  • Galaxy rotation & the RAR: Flat rotation curves and the radial acceleration relation are reproduced without particulate dark matter, via an entropic force sourced by $S_{\text{ent}}$.
  • Mass hierarchy & leptons: The electron, muon, and tau masses follow a log-quadratic pattern whose curvature is fixed by the same $g_{\text{share}}$, once the microscopic shell degeneracy is specified.
  • Black-hole thermodynamics: The Bekenstein–Hawking area law appears as the macroscopic limit of the same entanglement deficit scalar, with horizons acting as maximal-deficit surfaces.

How does it roughly work?

Microscopically, the theory models spacetime as a condensate of spin-3 tetrahedral quanta in a group-field or tensor-network–like framework. Each tetrahedron has four faces, each face carrying one of seven spin-$3$ projections $m = -3,\dots,+3$, with the constraint that all four faces are in distinct states. This gives a precise state-count:

1680 = 2 \times P(7,4) = 2 \times 7 \times 6 \times 5 \times 4

The factor of 2 accounts for the two chiral orientations of the tetrahedron; $P(7,4)$ counts distinct assignments of four faces drawn from seven spin states without repetition. The geometric wedge-sharing constant is then

g_{\text{share}} = \ln(1680) \approx 7.43.

Coarse-graining this condensate yields an effective scalar field $S_{\text{ent}}(x)$ and a continuum mass–information coupling $\kappa_m$. Running from the Planck scale (where black-hole entropy sets the UV boundary condition) down to the electron scale (where the 1-bit anchor fixes the IR) gives a tightly constrained $\kappa_m$ with a total exponent $5/2$ coming from area-law scaling (+2) plus three independent $+1/2$ channel-sharing contributions.

RAR / MOND-like Interpolation

On galactic scales, a two-horizon structure (local Rindler + cosmological) and a bosonic entropic response yield a specific radial-acceleration relation:

g_{\text{obs}} = \frac{g_{\text{bar}}}{1 - e^{-\sqrt{g_{\text{bar}}/a_0}}}, \quad a_0 = \frac{c H_0}{4\pi^2} \, g_{\text{share}}.

Once you assume (i) the two-horizon temperature, (ii) a single bosonic scale $a_0$, and (iii) linear entropic response to the Bose–Einstein occupation number, this shape is essentially forced: there is no remaining free interpolation parameter.

What's different from standard gravity / QFT?

Conceptually, this framework treats geometry and mass as emergent data structures built out of entanglement rather than as independent classical inputs. The Einstein tensor is replaced, at the EFT level, by functional derivatives of an entanglement-scalar action. The stress–energy of ordinary matter is reinterpreted as the back-reaction of changes in $S_{\text{ent}}$.

On the QFT side, the theory emphasizes operational entanglement entropy across spatial cuts rather than bare field amplitudes. In simple models (e.g., free Dirac fields), a single localized excitation generates a universal jump of $\ln 2$ in the reduced density matrix eigenvalues—providing a clean, state-independent "one bit" reference. Interacting toy models show that small mass shifts track small entanglement shifts, supporting the mass–information identification in the weak-coupling regime.

Spin-3 Condensate & Leptons

At the microscopic level, the medium is modeled as a spin-3 bosonic GFT field $\varphi$ (tetrahedra), while charged leptons are spin-3/2 fermionic defects $\psi$ living in that medium. A local four-fermion interaction of NJL type can be rewritten via a Hubbard–Stratonovich transformation into a composite boson $\Phi \sim \bar\psi\psi$ which condenses. Entropy and phase-space arguments favor the spin-3 channel ($J = 3$, dimension $2J+1=7$) over lower-spin pairings, naturally reproducing the spin-3 structure assumed in the wedge-sharing constant.

Where might it be wrong or incomplete?

The current program is deliberately minimal: it focuses on $G$, galaxy phenomenology, black-hole thermodynamics, and the lepton spectrum. Several important pieces remain open or only sketched:

  • A fully Lorentzian, dynamical spacetime treatment (beyond the static and quasi-static regimes) is not yet complete.
  • Neutrino masses, quark spectra, and full CKM/PMNS structure are not derived and are treated as future targets rather than predictions.
  • Cosmological back-reaction, structure formation, and lensing are constrained qualitatively by the framework but still require detailed comparison to data.
  • The microscopic identification $g_{\text{share}} = \ln(1680)$ is a strong, geometry-driven hypothesis, not yet a theorem of a fully specified GFT.

The Six-Paper Program

The detailed arguments are distributed across six papers:

  1. Entanglement Entropy: Unifying the Quantum Origins of Gravity, Mass, Time, and Cosmic Structure – defines $S_{\text{ent}}(x)$, $\kappa_m$, and the broad emergent picture.
  2. Supplement: Group Field / Tensor Underpinnings of the Entropic Scalar EFT – builds the discrete microstructure and coarse-graining flow.
  3. Derivations and Numerical Validations for the Entropic Scalar EFT – pins down the action, units, and numerics for $\kappa_m$ and galactic scaling.
  4. Theoretical Foundations of the Wedge-Sharing Constant $g_{\text{share}}$ – geometric derivation and random-walk persistence bounds.
  5. Entanglement–Scalar Derivation of $G$ (De Novo $G$) – shows how $G$ is fixed once $g_{\text{share}}$ is specified.
  6. Microstructure & Phenomenological Supplement – RAR / $a_0$ derivation, lepton shell Hamiltonian, Hawking-like checks, and spin-3 condensate consistency.
