Entanglement–Scalar Derivation of G (De Novo G)

Author: Jake Chinitz Type: De novo G derivation

This note shows how Newton's constant $G$ can be determined without gravitational data, once a dimensionless wedge-sharing constant $g_{\text{share}}$ is specified in the entanglement-scalar EFT. Identifying mass with information via $\kappa_m$ and using the electron as a one-bit anchor, the EFT's coarse-graining to macroscales yields a concrete, reproducible map $g_{\text{share}} \to G$ matching CODATA within uncertainties.
